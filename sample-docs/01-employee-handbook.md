# Lumen Trail Outfitters — Employee Handbook (2026 edition)

> Fictional company. All names, numbers and policies in this document are invented for testing a RAG system.

## 1. About the company

Lumen Trail Outfitters is an online store that sells hiking, camping and climbing gear. It was founded in 2017 in Bogotá by two climbers, Mariana Ortiz and Felipe Ramos. In 2026 the company has 84 employees across three teams: Commerce, Logistics and Platform Engineering. The headquarters are in Bogotá and the main warehouse is in Funza, Cundinamarca.

The company's mission is "gear that lasts longer than the trip". Every product sold must have at least a 2-year warranty.

## 2. Working hours and remote work

- The standard work week is 42 hours, Monday to Friday.
- Core hours are 9:30 to 15:30 (Bogotá time). Meetings should be scheduled inside core hours.
- Office-based roles can work remotely up to **3 days per week**. Warehouse roles in Funza are fully on-site.
- Fully remote contracts require approval from the team director and the People team.
- Every employee receives a one-time home-office stipend of **COP 1,200,000** in the first month.

## 3. Time off

- Employees get **18 working days** of paid vacation per year, plus Colombian public holidays.
- After 5 years at the company, vacation increases to **22 working days**.
- Up to **5 unused days** can be carried over to the next year. They expire on March 31.
- Vacation requests of more than 3 consecutive days must be submitted at least **15 calendar days** in advance in the HR portal ("Basecamp").
- Every employee gets their **birthday off** as an extra paid day.
- Sick leave requires a medical certificate after 2 consecutive days.

## 4. Benefits

- **Gear discount:** 35% off all products in the store, up to COP 3,000,000 per year.
- **Learning budget:** USD 800 per year for courses, books and certifications. Unused budget does not carry over.
- **Outdoor day:** once per quarter, each team takes a paid day for an outdoor activity. The company pays transport and food.
- **Health:** complementary health insurance (medicina prepagada) for the employee, covered 70% by the company.

## 5. Performance reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in **June and December**. Each review has a self-assessment, peer feedback from 2 colleagues and a 1:1 with the manager. Salary adjustments are applied in **January** only.
