## 2. Choosing a tent

- **Páramo** tents are for trekking up to about **4,000 m** in rain and wind, but not snow. The Páramo 1 is the lightest tent in the catalog.
- **Nevado** and **Cocuy** tents are 4-season tents for high mountains and snow. They support a snow load of up to **40 kg/m²**.
- The **Cocuy 3** is the only model with **two doors and two vestibules**, which makes it the recommended tent for expeditions with gear.

## 3. Materials

- Rainfly: 20D ripstop nylon with silicone coating, water column **3,000 mm** (Páramo) and **5,000 mm** (Nevado, Cocuy).
- Floor: 40D nylon, water column **8,000 mm** on all models.
- All seams are taped at the factory.

## 4. Care

- Never store a tent wet. Dry it completely within **48 hours** to avoid mold.
- Clean with water and mild soap only. Never use a washing machine.
- Re-apply silicone seam sealer every **2 seasons** of heavy use.
- Damaged poles can be replaced through the repair workshop. A replacement pole section costs **COP 38,000**.

## 5. Frequently asked questions

**Can I use the Páramo 2 on a glacier?** No. It is a 3-season tent and isn't designed for snow loads. Use the Nevado 2 or Cocuy 3.

**Which tent is best for a solo trek in the Andes in the rainy season?** The Páramo 1: it's the lightest and handles rain and wind up to about 4,000 m.
