# Lumen Trail Outfitters — Travel and Expense Policy

> Fictional company. All names, numbers and policies in this document are invented for testing a RAG system.

## 1. Booking travel

- All business trips must be approved by the team director **before** booking.
- Flights are booked through the travel agency **"Rumbo Claro"**, at least **10 days** in advance for domestic trips and **21 days** for international trips.
- Economy class is the default. Business class is allowed only for flights longer than **8 hours**.

## 2. Daily allowance (per diem)

| Destination | Meals per day | Hotel per night (max) |
|---|---|---|
| Colombia (domestic) | COP 120,000 | COP 350,000 |
| Latin America | USD 60 | USD 150 |
| USA and Europe | USD 85 | USD 220 |

Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## 3. Expense reports

- Submit expense reports in the HR portal ("Basecamp") within **15 days** after the trip.
- Every expense over **COP 50,000** needs a receipt.
- Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll, on the **15th or the last day** of the month.

## 4. Company card

Employees who travel more than **4 times per year** can request a company credit card with a monthly limit of **COP 8,000,000**. Personal expenses on the company card are not allowed.
